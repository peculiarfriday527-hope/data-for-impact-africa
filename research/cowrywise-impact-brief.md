Cowrywise: Data for Financial Impact in Nigeria

1. Who are they?

Cowrywise is a Nigerian financial technology company that provides digital savings and investment services. It helps people save towards financial goals, develop better financial habits, and access investment opportunities through its digital platform.

2. What data do they use?

Cowrywise uses customer feedback and survey data to understand how its services affect users' financial lives. This includes customers' reported ability to save, financial discipline, financial stability, access to additional funds, and changes in their quality of life.

A customer impact study conducted in partnership with 60 Decibels gathered information about customers' experiences and the changes they reported after using Cowrywise.

3. What changed because of it?

The impact study reported that:

- 78% of customers reported improved quality of life.
- 69% reported increased financial stability.
- 37% reported improved financial discipline.
- 32% reported improved ability to save.
- 11% reported access to additional funds.

These findings suggest that digital financial services can support improvements in people's financial habits and well-being.

Source

BFA Global/Catalyst Fund and 60 Decibels, Cowrywise Impact Performance Report (2021).
