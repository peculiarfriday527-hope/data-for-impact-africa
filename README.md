🌍 Data for Impact Africa

About This Project

Data for Impact Africa is a research project exploring how organizations across Africa use data, technology, and insights to address real-world challenges and improve people's lives.

This project was created as part of my Week 1 Data Analytics Impact Lab.

🎯 Project Objective

To explore how organizations collect, analyze, and use data to measure impact, support decision-making, and solve problems affecting individuals and communities.

📌 Case Study: Cowrywise

Organization: Cowrywise
Industry: Financial Technology (FinTech)
Country: Nigeria

Cowrywise is a Nigerian financial technology company providing digital savings and investment services that help people develop financial habits and work towards their financial goals.

1. Who Are They?

Cowrywise provides digital savings and investment services, helping individuals save money, build financial discipline, and access investment opportunities through its platform.

2. What Data Do They Use?

Cowrywise uses customer feedback and survey data to understand how its services affect users' financial lives.

The data includes customers' reported:

- Ability to save
- Financial discipline
- Financial stability
- Access to additional funds
- Changes in quality of life

3. What Changed Because of It?

A customer impact study conducted in partnership with 60 Decibels reported the following outcomes:

Reported Outcome| Percentage
Improved quality of life| 78%
Increased financial stability| 69%
Improved financial discipline| 37%
Improved ability to save| 32%
Access to additional funds| 11%

These findings suggest that digital financial services can support improvements in customers' financial habits and well-being.

Note: These are customer-reported outcomes from the impact study and do not necessarily establish that Cowrywise alone caused the reported improvements.

💡 Key Learning

This research helped me understand that data analytics goes beyond numbers, spreadsheets, and dashboards. Data can also help organizations understand people's experiences, measure outcomes, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions.

As I continue learning data analytics, I want to understand how to turn data into meaningful insights that can contribute to solving real-world problems.
